How to Request

Steps to Delete Your Account

To request deletion of your Quizora account and associated data, follow these steps:

  1. 1
    Send an email to gulrehman1324@gmail.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  2. 2
    Include the email address associated with your Quizora account so we can locate your data
  3. 3
    We will verify your identity and send a confirmation email within 2 business days
  4. 4
    Your account and data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your confirmed request

What Happens

Data Deleted vs. Retained

Here is a breakdown of what happens to your data upon a confirmed deletion request:

Data Type Action Timeframe
Account info (email, username) Deleted Within 30 days
Quiz scores & progress Deleted Within 30 days
Leaderboard entries Deleted Within 30 days
Crash logs & diagnostics Auto-expires Within 90 days
Anonymized analytics data Retained (anonymized) No personal link
