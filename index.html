How Quizora collects, uses, and protects your information

Last Updated: 26 April 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Quizora ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Quizora mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

01

Information We Collect

Quizora is a simple quiz application. We collect only minimal information necessary to operate and improve the App.

Device & Usage Data

We may collect basic technical data such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage statistics (quiz attempts, scores, feature usage)
  • Crash logs and performance data

Optional User Data

If the App includes login or leaderboard features, we may collect:

  • Username or display name
  • Email address (if login is enabled)

We Do NOT Collect

Financial Information Personal Documents Location Tracking Camera / Microphone
02

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core quiz functionality
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Track scores, progress, or leaderboards (if enabled)
  • Fix bugs and ensure stability
  • Prevent misuse or abuse of the App
We do not sell or trade your personal data to any third parties — ever.
03

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

Consent Legitimate Interests Contract
  • Consent – when you agree to optional features
  • Legitimate Interests – improving and maintaining the App
  • Contract – delivering core quiz services
04

Sharing Your Information

We may share limited data with trusted service providers only for operational purposes, such as:

  • Firebase / backend hosting services
  • Analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase)
  • Crash reporting services

All third parties are required to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide.

05

Data Retention

  • Usage and analytics data: up to 12–24 months
  • User accounts (if any): until deleted by the user
  • Crash logs: up to 90 days or as required for debugging
06

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us:

✉️

Send your request to your@email.com and we'll respond promptly.

07

Children's Privacy

Quizora is safe for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, it will be deleted immediately.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us right away.

08

Security

We use appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • Secure servers and encrypted connections
  • Restricted access controls
  • Regular security reviews
However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continuously work to minimize risks.
09

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that collect limited data for analytics, crash reporting, and cloud storage. These services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Google / Firebase Privacy Policy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
